Australian Wildlife Spotting

Here in Australia, there are a range of wildlife related activities Australian can participate in, one of which is wildlife spotting. Australia is home to a diverse set of wildlife, ranging from icon mammals to rare amphibians. The opportunity to explore and spot these animals are dependent on the location, time of day and year, and the animal’s overall rarity.

Whether it be observing kangaroos at dawn, listening for nocturnal calls in the rainforest, or scanning coastal waters for migrating whales, wildlife spotting invites all Australians no matter their age to engage deeply with the environment and appreciate the remarkable biodiversity that defines the continent.
